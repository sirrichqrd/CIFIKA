05

Club Rules and Dojo Etiquette

All members are expected to observe the Club Rules and Dojo Kun.

The following rules apply:

  1. Members must bow upon entering and leaving the dojo and show respect to instructors and fellow students.
  2. Clean, correct karate-gi must be worn; keep nails trimmed and remove jewellery.
  3. Punctuality is required. If late, wait at the dojo edge and seek permission to join.
  4. Follow instructor directions at all times. Do not attempt techniques beyond your level without supervision.
  5. No sparring or contact outside supervised class. Control is paramount.
  6. Maintain hygiene and personal conduct befitting martial arts.
  7. Fees must be paid by the due date. Membership requires TTOSR affiliation.
  8. Advise the instructor of any injury or illness before training.
  9. Children must be supervised before and after class; guardian pickup is required for under 12.
  10. There is zero tolerance for bullying, discrimination or unsafe behaviour.
  11. Dojo equipment must be cared for and returned.
  12. The Club may amend its rules with notice. Continued training constitutes acceptance of notified changes.

06

Dojo Kun

Members acknowledge the Dojo Kun as the guiding principles of training:

  1. I shall strive for the perfection of character.
  2. I shall defend the path of truth.
  3. I shall foster the spirit of effort.
  4. I shall honour the principles of etiquette.
  5. I shall guard against impetuous courage.

Members are expected to demonstrate respect, discipline, self-control, effort and good character both inside and outside the dojo.

07

Training Participation and Safety

Karate is a physical martial art involving physical exertion, contact, movement and inherent risks.

These risks may include bruises, strains, sprains, falls, fractures and other physical injuries.

By participating, you acknowledge that:

  • Participation is voluntary.
  • You should disclose relevant medical conditions, allergies and injuries.
  • You must follow instructor directions and safety procedures.
  • You should not attempt techniques beyond your level without supervision.
  • You must inform an instructor if you feel unwell or sustain an injury.
  • You should seek appropriate medical advice where necessary before participating in training.

For children, the parent or guardian is responsible for providing relevant health and emergency information.

The Club will take reasonable steps to promote a safe training environment. Nothing in these Terms excludes liability where exclusion is prohibited by applicable law.
