PRIVACY POLICY

City Fitness Karate Club (CIFIKA)

Effective Date: 7 November 2026 Last Updated: 7 November 2026

01

Introduction

City Fitness Karate Club (CIFIKA), referred to in this Privacy Policy as "CIFIKA", "the Club", "we", "us" or "our", respects the privacy of its members, students, parents, guardians and visitors.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process and protect personal information when you:

  • Visit our website at https://cifika.ttosr.com/
  • Complete our online membership registration.
  • Create or access a CIFIKA Member Portal account.
  • Participate in karate training, grading, competitions, events and other club activities.
  • Communicate with the Club through our available contact channels.

Our registration and membership systems are supported by TTOSR.

By using our services or submitting your registration, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required, we will request it separately.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on your relationship with the Club and the services you use, we may collect the following information.

A. Personal and Membership Information

  • Full name and preferred name.
  • Date of birth.
  • Nationality and gender.
  • Telephone number and email address.
  • Residential address and city/state.
  • Membership reference number (CFK-xxxxx).
  • Membership status and training category.

B. Parent and Guardian Information

For children and members represented by a parent or guardian, we may collect:

  • Parent or guardian's full name.
  • Relationship to the member.
  • Telephone number and email address.
  • Residential address, where applicable.
  • Confirmation of parental or guardian responsibility and consent.

C. Emergency and Health Information

For training safety and emergency preparedness, we may collect:

  • Emergency contact name and relationship.
  • Primary and alternative emergency telephone numbers.
  • Relevant medical conditions and allergies.
  • Injury or surgery recovery information.
  • Relevant action plans or training modifications.

Health information is treated with particular care and used for appropriate training safety and emergency purposes.

D. Martial Arts and Training Information

We may collect:

  • Previous martial arts experience.
  • Martial arts disciplines and styles.
  • Previous clubs, dojos and instructors.
  • Current or previous ranks and grades.
  • Years of training and previous training history.
  • Training objectives and development goals.
  • Attendance, grading results, scores and certificates.

E. Photographs and Profile Information

We may process:

  • Passport photographs submitted during registration.
  • Profile pictures provided through Google sign-in.
  • Photographs or videos taken during club training, events and activities, subject to your media consent choice.

03

How We Collect Information

We collect information through:

  • Online membership registration forms.
  • Member Portal account creation and authentication.
  • Google sign-in, when selected by the user.
  • Parent or guardian submissions.
  • Staff-assisted registration.
  • Training attendance and grading activities.
  • Direct communication with members or guardians.

When Google sign-in is used, Google may provide your name, email address and profile picture, depending on the permissions granted.

04

How We Use Your Information

CIFIKA uses personal information for legitimate club administration, membership services, safety and communication purposes, including:

  1. Processing membership applications and issuing CIFIKA member IDs.
  2. Verifying membership and linking member records to portal accounts.
  3. Managing student, parent and guardian accounts.
  4. Maintaining attendance and training records.
  5. Monitoring belt progression and grading eligibility.
  6. Recording grading assessments, results and certificates.
  7. Managing training schedules, membership status and session balances.
  8. Processing absence requests and membership enquiries.
  9. Communicating club announcements, events, seminars and relevant updates.
  10. Supporting emergency response and safeguarding.
  11. Meeting relevant affiliation and administrative requirements, including TTOSR affiliation.
  12. Improving the operation and security of our registration and membership systems.

We do not sell members' personal information.

05

How Information Is Stored and Processed

CIFIKA uses digital systems and service providers to support its operations.

These may include:

  • Supabase: Authentication, account management and membership portal data.
  • Google: Authentication when you choose Google sign-in.
  • SheetDB: Processing and synchronisation of membership registration records.
  • TTOSR: Technology and operational support for CIFIKA systems.
  • WhatsApp: Communication and application submission when a member chooses to share their registration PDF through WhatsApp.

Registration drafts may also be saved in your browser's local storage. If you use a shared device, other people with access to that browser or device may be able to access saved drafts.

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-party providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

06

Information Sharing

We may share relevant information:

  • With authorised CIFIKA administrators and instructors who need it for their responsibilities.
  • With TTOSR for relevant technology, administration and affiliation support.
  • With relevant martial arts associations or affiliation bodies where necessary for membership, grading or affiliation.
  • With appropriate insurance or safety-related service providers where necessary.
  • With technology providers that help us operate authentication, registration and membership services.
  • Where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect the safety of a member or another person.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information for advertising purposes.

07

Children and Parental Consent

CIFIKA welcomes children and young students.

Where a child or minor registers for training:

  • A parent or legal guardian may be required to provide their details.
  • The parent or guardian is responsible for providing accurate information and appropriate consent.
  • Guardian contact information may be used for membership, safeguarding, emergency and communication purposes.
  • Parents or guardians may manage linked child accounts through the Member Portal.
  • We take particular care when handling children's personal information and photographs.

Parents and guardians may contact the Club to review or request correction of information relating to their child.

08

Photography and Media Consent

CIFIKA may take photographs or videos during training sessions, events, seminars and club activities.

Media consent is collected separately during registration.

You may choose:

  • Yes: Consent to club photographs and videos for training and promotional purposes.
  • No: Do not consent to the use of your photographs or videos for those purposes.

Media consent is separate from the general membership and privacy consent.

You may contact the Club to withdraw your consent for future promotional use. Withdrawal does not automatically require the removal of material already lawfully published or distributed, but we will consider reasonable requests.

For children, the relevant parent or guardian provides the media consent.

09

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including:

  • Active membership administration.
  • Training, attendance and grading history.
  • Safety and emergency records.
  • Affiliation and administrative requirements.
  • Resolving disputes and meeting applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Saved browser drafts may remain on the device until cleared by the user or removed through the browser's storage controls.

10

Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

  • Know what personal information CIFIKA holds about you.
  • Access your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of information where appropriate.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Object to certain uses of your personal information.
  • Request clarification about how your information is used.

Requests involving a child may be made by their parent or legal guardian.

Some information may need to be retained where required for legitimate administrative, safety or legal purposes.

11

Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website and portal may link to or use third-party services, including Google and WhatsApp.

These services operate independently and have their own privacy policies and terms. CIFIKA does not control how these third parties process information under their own policies.

We encourage users to review the relevant policies before using those services.

12

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, systems, operational requirements or applicable legal obligations change.

The updated version will be published on our website with a revised "Last Updated" date.
