SECTION I — CLUB RULES (12)
Scroll to read
  1. Members must bow upon entering and leaving the dojo and show respect to instructors and fellow students.
  2. Clean, correct karate-gi must be worn; keep nails trimmed and remove jewellery.
  3. Punctuality is required. If late, wait at dojo edge and seek permission to join.
  4. Follow instructor directions at all times. Do not attempt techniques beyond your level without supervision.
  5. No sparring or contact outside supervised class. Control is paramount.
  6. Maintain hygiene and personal conduct befitting martial arts.
  7. Fees must be paid by due date. Membership requires TTOSR affiliation.
  8. Advise instructor of any injury or illness before training.
  9. Children must be supervised before/after class; guardian pickup required for under 12.
  10. Zero tolerance for bullying, discrimination or unsafe behaviour.
  11. Dojo equipment must be cared for and returned.
  12. Club may amend rules with notice; continued training constitutes acceptance.
